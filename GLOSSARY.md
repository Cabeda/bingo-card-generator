# Bingo event

## Language

**Card**:
A numbered 90-ball bingo grid with three rows of nine cells and five numbers per row.
A printed card keeps its original numbers throughout an event.

**Printed sheet**:
An A4 page containing one to four cards in their original order.
Every card, including its heading and footer, fits entirely on that page.

**Print preview**:
A scaled view of the first printed sheet, showing the same cards, layout and theme as the printed deck.
